## Caption structures observed

The deck includes direct quotes, POV conditions, workplace grievances, family observations, confessions, role collisions and exaggerated counterfactuals. Most illustrated slides pair a setup outside the image with reaction or dialogue inside it. That is a strong form for this source, and only one possible relationship for the general skill.

## Boundaries of this evidence

Some slides are text-only and the "laptop as best friend" idea recurs. Some visual references are copyrighted or culturally narrow. Do not convert those source quirks into mandatory generation rules. The deck does not provide engagement metrics, publication dates, licensing evidence or proof that an audience recognizes each template.

## Transfer test

Take the mechanism, change the people, tools, consequences and visual form. If a new dentist, logistics manager or gamer example still sounds like an agency designer wearing a different name, reject it as overfitting.

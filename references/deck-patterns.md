# Deck-Derived Humor Patterns & Inspiration

**Canonical Inspiration Source**: [Meme Marketing Master Deck (Google Slides)](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Dnxy0wxP8G4k_9LToeDuopg-hcqxcg3jg7gIb4u_K2A/edit?usp=drive_link)

The deck holds about 95 live examples from two families of Egyptian content:

1. **Image memes** (about 50 slides): a caption in the post text, a reaction still from Egyptian cinema, series, cartoons or animal photos, and one bold line on the image. Two brand pages appear: a meme-marketing course page (agency, design, content and AI audience) and a lifestyle page aimed at young Egyptian women (family, marriage pressure, siblings).
2. **Relatable text posts** (about 45 slides): tweet-style cards, Facebook text-background posts and Snap-style mood cards. These are covered in depth in [relatable-posts.md](relatable-posts.md).

The deck is the gold standard for tone. The agent studies **how** each example works and transfers the mechanism. It never copies an example into a client deliverable unless the user asks for that exact line.

---

## What the deck teaches, in eight rules

1. **Every meme starts from a lived situation.** The captions read like something a person said out loud today: "لما يجيلي تعديل على ديزاين قديم:", "لما اسيب شركة كنت شغال فيها...".
2. **Three layers, three jobs.** Caption = cause. Face = emotion. On-image line = twist spoken by the character. See the anatomy in [humor-mechanics.md](humor-mechanics.md).
3. **Money is always a real number.** "1200 جنيه", "2700 جنيه", "1850 جنيه", "16 ساعة". Exact figures make the injustice concrete.
4. **Cinema memory does half the work.** Lines such as "الراجل دا كريم اوي يا بابا", "كلمني صنايعي لصنايعي", "ايدك بقيت بترعش يا قنصل", "خودني معاك يا سي دوني" bring the whole original scene with them.
5. **The brand shows up rarely and laughs at itself.** Out of roughly 30 course-page memes, only about 4 mention the course, and each one makes the course owner the victim (tired from grading, students sharing material, the course suggested shyly as the "embarrassing solution").
6. **Wordplay is visual.** "قميص مشجر" shows a dog in a floral shirt next to freshly cut trees (مشجر as floral and as "full of trees"). "الـ Ai الوحيد اللي أعرفه" shows the Adobe Illustrator logo.
7. **Domestic humour is affectionate.** The mother, the younger brother, the strict father and the neighbour are drawn with love. The joke is on the situation.
8. **Branding is a small badge.** A corner logo on every image; no slogans, no links, no CTA.

## How the agent uses this deck

1. **Extract the mechanism, then forget the words.** Log which mechanism and relation an example uses; write fresh lines for the client.
2. **Transfer through the Collision Engine.** Feed the deck insight into [humor-engine.md](humor-engine.md) Stage 2 with the client's world as the new domain. Example transfers:
   - Fintech: "20 ريل بـ 2700" becomes the prospect who chose an unencrypted Google Sheet over your SOC2 tool.
   - DevOps: "الـ Ai فصل" becomes "AWS console وقع ولازم تفتكر الـ CLI syntax".
   - Clinic: "أمي والعريس" becomes "أمي لما الدكتور قال محتاجة تقويم ورفضت عشان الصور".
3. **Keep the cadence.** Egyptian texture ("هو أنا بهزر معاك؟", "جايب هاتريك", "الكرباج عليك") stays spoken. Never sanitise it into formal Arabic unless the register hijack is the joke.
4. **Respect rights.** Cinema stills are `unverified_reference` until licensed. Always pair them with an original fallback that keeps the same emotion and on-image line.

# Relatable posts ("اه دا انا")

A relatable post is a short first-person line, usually text on a plain background or a screenshot-style card, that makes the reader think "this is exactly me" or "this is exactly my sister". It does not need a reaction image. The words carry everything.

In the deck these are the tweet-style cards (dark card, avatar, name), the Facebook text-background posts, and the Snap-style yellow text on black. They routinely earn more shares than image memes because sharing them costs nothing socially: the sender gets to say something about themselves without writing it.

Use this reference when the user asks for relatable posts, "بوستات الناس تقول عليها دا انا", tweet cards, text posts, Facebook status posts, or a mix of memes and text posts. The route is CREATE or BATCH with `content_type: relatable_post`.

---

## Why people share them

| Driver | What happens in the reader's head | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Self-recognition (debugging theory) | "I caught myself doing this" produces a small laugh | The line must describe a behaviour, with the reader as the one doing it |
| Pluralistic ignorance relief | "I thought only I did this" becomes "everyone does this" | Pick private, slightly embarrassing habits |
| Identity signalling (Berger's social currency) | Sharing says "this is who I am" | The line should flatter or forgive the sharer a little |
| Proxy speech | "I can't say this to them, so I'll share it" | Arrow posts aimed at a friend, sibling, partner, boss |
| Tagging | "@سارة دي انتي" | Name a recognisable type of person the reader can tag |

Two share modes. Every post should be designed for one of them, and the best ones allow both:

- **Mirror share**: the reader posts it on their own story because it describes them. Test: "Would someone repost this with '😂 انا'?"
- **Arrow share**: the reader sends it to one person because it describes them. Test: "Can you name who they tag?"

---

## The Barnum sweet spot

The Forer (Barnum) effect shows that people accept vague statements as personal descriptions. Relatable posts use a sharper version: the line must **sound** like a specific personal confession and **be** common enough that most of the audience has done it.

| Too vague | Sweet spot | Too niche |
|---|---|---|
| "بحب النوم" | "النوم كارفلي" | "بنام الساعة 3:17 بالظبط بعد الحلقة التالتة من مسلسل تركي" |
| "أهلي بيضايقوني" | "هناك ثلاثة أشياء يأتون بلا موعد: الصحة، الرزق، أختي وعيالها" | A story about one specific aunt |
| "بتوتر في المواصلات" | "نزلت طنطا ووقفت تاكسي فبيقولي على فين (قعدت 5 دقايق بفكر في كلمة سيرباي)" | A full route description |

Check: the detail should be specific (one word, number or object) and the behaviour behind it should be universal.

## The twelve relatable formulas (from the deck)

Each formula lists the structure, a deck example, and how to transfer it to a brand's audience. Examples are Egyptian Arabic unless marked.

### 1. Private-habit confession
Structure: "أي [trigger] بت[do something disproportionate]"
Deck: "أي جملة بتبدأ بكلمة طاجن بتستحوذ على 95% من تركيزي"
Transfer (performance marketer): "أي إشعار فيه كلمة Purchase بيصحيني من أعمق نوم"

### 2. Brain math glitch
Structure: "هو انا ليه عندي قناعة ان [wrong belief the reader shares]"
Deck: "هو انا ليه عندي قناعة ان الربع مليون جنيه أكتر من الـ 300 ألف جنيه"
Transfer (e-commerce owner): "هو انا ليه مقتنع ان 199 جنيه أرخص بكتير من 200"

### 3. Tech vocabulary for life
Structure: life problem + one software verb (sign out, refresh, restart, update, storage full)
Deck: "أنا دخلت في مسؤوليات أكبر مني بالغلط، ازاي اعمل sign out" / "الواحد محتاج refresh شغفيا ونفسيا ومزاجيا"
Transfer (students): "عايز أعمل clear cache لكل اللي ذاكرته قبل الامتحان بساعة وأنزله تاني"

### 4. Register hijack (formal voice, trivial pain)
Structure: فصحى proverb, news sign-off, official announcement or everyday دعاء formula applied to a small daily pain
Deck: "مساء الخير: المواصلات عملالكوا سربرايز تحفة بكره، تصبحوا على خير" / "اللهم عجل بانتهاء العام الدراسي"
Transfer (agency team): "بيان هام: الكلاينت شاف الديزاين وقال ملاحظة صغيرة. نسألكم الدعاء"
Care: keep religious formulas to common everyday phrases, never quote Quran or hadith for comedy, and skip this register for conservative Gulf clients unless approved.

### 5. Rule-of-three break
Structure: a list of two serious items, then a third personal item that derails it
Deck: "هناك ثلاثة أشياء يأتون بلا موعد: الصحة، الرزق، أختي وعيالها"
Transfer (freelancer): "الحاجات اللي مبتخلصش: الدنيا، الدين، وتعديلات الكلاينت ده"

### 6. Family field report
Structure: one family member's predictable behaviour, reported like a discovery, often with a modern tool
Deck: "ماما بقت بتكسل تدعيلي فبتبعتلي ريلز لأمهات بتدعي لعيالها" / "بابا دخل عليا الأوضة لقاني سهران فقالي ايه يا معلم مافيش كلية بكرا ولا ايه (انا متخرج أصلا)"
Transfer (remote workers): "بابا لسه مقتنع ان الشغل من البيت يعني اجازة مفتوحة وبيطلب مني أجيب عيش الساعة 11 الصبح"

### 7. Energy budget
Structure: social or emotional effort measured in absurd units
Deck: "ما احنا لسه متكلمين من ٩ أيام هي شغلانة" / "نفسي اخد من وقتكم 15 ساعة اشتكي فيهم"
Transfer (B2B): "لسه رادد على إيميل امبارح، عايز إجازة اسبوع"

### 8. Life-stage fatigue
Structure: "كان ممكن [old energetic behaviour] وأنا [younger age] لكن حاليا [tired]"
Deck: "كان ممكن أناقشك وناخد وندي في الكلام مثلا وأنا 21 سنة لكن حاليا صعب"
Transfer (new parents): "كان ممكن أسهر معاكم للفجر وأنا 24، دلوقتي بنام وأنا بقول تصبحوا على خير"

### 9. Cliché made literal
Structure: take a self-help or emotional cliché and apply a real-world constraint
Deck: "نفسي ابدأ حياة جديدة.. بس القديمة لسه عليها أقساط" / "الفريست ستيب عشان تصالح امك أصعب من أي فريست ستيب هتاخدها في حياتك"
Transfer (fintech): "قررت أعيش اللحظة، اللحظة طلعت عليها رسوم تحويل"

### 10. Mock authority
Structure: the writer speaks like a principal, judge or manager about a tiny social behaviour
Deck: "الناس اللي بتلغي الأدد وبتبعته تاني، شوف والله لو جبتلي ولي أمرك ماهقبله"
Transfer (community page): "اللي بيكتب 'ممكن السعر' وهو شايف السعر في الصورة، يتفضل على مكتبي"

### 11. Cross-market translation card
Structure: one concept, three flags or three dialects, the last one is the most honest
Deck: "🇺🇸 Bad news. / 🇸🇦 أخبار سيئة ومشكلة كبيرة / 🇪🇬 صاحية"
Transfer (Gulf-Egypt agency): "🇺🇸 Deadline / 🇸🇦 الموعد النهائي / 🇪🇬 امبارح"
Care: affectionate toward every country involved; the joke targets the shared situation, never a nationality.

### 12. Emotional proxy (mood card)
Structure: slow, line-by-line sad or wistful statement, yellow text on black, one word per line for weight
Deck: "يؤسفني / برودي / إتجاه / أكثر علاقة / كنت أتمناها / لنهاية عمري" / "بس عارف بدأت تحس ان الشخص اللي انت بتحب تتكلم معاه مبقاش حابب يتكلم معاك"
Use: these spread as indirect messages to a partner or friend. Brands rarely use them as-is. The transferable part is the **format** (slow vertical rhythm) applied to a light topic: "وحشاني / حاجة / لو / رجعت تاني / مش / هقبل بيها" can be re-used for a product that was discontinued and is back.
Care: never use for grief, mental illness or real breakups tied to a brand.

## Writing rules

- First person, present tense, spoken dialect.
- One idea per post. If you need "و" twice, split into two posts.
- Put the turn in the last 2 to 5 words, or in a parenthesis at the end "(انا متخرج أصلا)".
- Keep natural typing texture for the dialect: "فـ", "دا/ده", "..", "!!", a single emoji at most. Avoid perfect punctuation; it reads like an ad.
- Numbers must be plausible lived numbers or clearly absurd; never a statistic presented as research.
- No product name in the line, unless the product is naturally part of the habit (for example "Ads Manager", "Canva", "طلبات"). The brand identity sits in the card design and the account posting it.
- No CTA, no hashtag, no link inside the card.

## Visual formats for relatable posts

| Format | Look | Best for | Build note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark tweet card | Dark navy/black card, round avatar, display name and handle, white text | Confessions, family reports, brain glitches | `meme-craft render --template text-card --style dark` |
| Snap mood card | Black background, centred yellow text, one phrase per line | Emotional proxy, slow reveals | `--style black-gold` |
| Facebook text background | Bright orange or gradient block, dark bold text | Family field reports, rants | `--style orange` |
| Plain screenshot of your own post | Real screenshot from the brand's own account | Posts that already performed organically | Use real numbers only |

Identity and honesty rules for cards:

- Use the brand's own page name and avatar, or a clearly fictional persona that the brand owns. Never mimic a real person's account, verified badge or handle.
- Never fabricate like, comment or share counts. Leave engagement rows out of rendered cards, or use real numbers from a real post.
- A small watermark (page logo in a corner) is enough branding. The deck uses a small badge in the top-left corner on every meme.

## Batch mix

Relatable posts and image memes feed different share behaviours. For a monthly calendar, a practical default mix is:

- 50% image memes (reaction still, original scene, labels)
- 30% relatable text posts (mirror share)
- 20% arrow posts designed to be tagged or sent (family, partner, colleague types)

Adjust with the taste profile after the first month of real engagement data.

## Gates specific to relatable posts

Run these in addition to the general gates in [bineval-gates.md](bineval-gates.md):

1. **"اه دا انا" test**: would 6 in 10 readers say it describes them or someone close?
2. **Mirror or arrow**: can you state whether it is reposted (mirror) or sent/tagged (arrow), and to whom?
3. **Barnum sweet spot**: one specific detail, universal behaviour.
4. **Turn at the end**: the last words change or sharpen the meaning.
5. **Voice honesty**: posted from the brand's own identity; no fake account, no fake counts.
